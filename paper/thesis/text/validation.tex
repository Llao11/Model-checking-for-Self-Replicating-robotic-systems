
For the validation of  properties checked in a previous chapter
and for demonstrating the feasibility of 
the counterexample-guided control approach, we complement the formal analysis 
with an experimental simulation platform. The platform is realized in 
the Gazebo environment, which provides a physics-based simulation of 
the modular robot and its interaction with voxel-like blocks. The robot 
is controlled through ROS2 nodes, enabling modular and distributed control 
strategies. By comparing the outcomes of formal verification with simulation 
results, we aim to assess both the soundness and the practical utility of 
model-checking-based methods for modular self-replicating robots.
