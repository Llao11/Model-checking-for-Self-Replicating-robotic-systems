
\section{Use of AI for publications search}

\textbf{System:} Perplexity

\textbf{URL:} https://www.perplexity.ai/

\textbf{Date of use:} June-August 2025

\textbf{Purpose:} Search for related scientific papers
during the preparation of Background chapter.

\textbf{Description:}
During the preparation process, AI was used to create a list of relevant scientific articles,
working groups and published results regarding Self-Replication systems and
implementation of Formal Methods to Robotics.

\textbf{Result:}
The use of AI has accelerated the search process and helped to reflect the entire spectrum of
the current state of research in a field of SRRS and application of Formal Methods. 
Around 20\% of scientific sources were found by this AI tool.
The results and found publications were used in Chapter \ref{ch:background}.

\section{Use of AI for rephrasing, grammar correction and synonym search}

\textbf{System:} ChatGPT-5

\textbf{URL:} https://chatgpt.com/

\textbf{Date of use:} August - October 2025

\textbf{Description:} AI was used to select synonyms and alternative formulations
for my own sentences, and to correct grammar. The thoughts and arguments remained mine; AI only
helped to find a clearer or less repetitive way of expressing them. This made
the text more smooth and reduced the number of stylistic repetitions.

\textbf{Result:} The main meaning was preserved, but the wording became clearer
and more varied, which improved readability and the overall quality of the
work.
